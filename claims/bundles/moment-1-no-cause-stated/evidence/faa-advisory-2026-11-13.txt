FAA NATIONWIDE GROUND STOP ADVISORY — Friday, November 13, 2026
Text of the Federal Aviation Administration Command Center advisory, as
published verbatim in contemporaneous reporting (northjersey.com,
"FAA lifts ground stop nationwide after hours of delays, cancellations,"
retrieved 2026-11-14). A United States government statement; the words
are the FAA's.

Due to extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety. Attention all aircraft operators, by order of the Federal Aviation Command Center all airports are ground stopped. All departing traffic should remain ground stopped until further notice. Inbound international flights will be managed on a case by case basis.
